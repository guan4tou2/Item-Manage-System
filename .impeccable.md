## Design Context

### Users

IMS serves households and small teams that need to find, track, lend, restock, and audit physical items across rooms, locations, warehouses, and shared groups. Users are often doing quick operational tasks: add an item, scan a label, locate something, check low stock, see who borrowed an item, or review recent inventory activity.

### Brand Personality

The interface should feel calm, precise, and trustworthy. It should help users make fast decisions without looking like a consumer marketing page. The tone is practical and content-first, with clear Traditional Chinese and English copy.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the existing v2 direction: Notion / Linear inspired, medium-high information density, generous but controlled whitespace, slate neutrals, deep purple as the primary action color, light and dark mode support, shadcn/ui primitives, Tailwind tokens, and restrained borders instead of heavy shadows.

### Design Principles

- Prioritize scanability: dashboards, tables, filters, and cards should expose status and next actions quickly.
- Keep operations efficient: common actions such as scan, add, search, edit, favorite, export, and retry should stay easy to reach on desktop and mobile.
- Make empty, loading, and error states useful by offering a clear next step.
- Preserve mobile capability: do not hide important functions on small screens; adapt the layout and action placement.
- Stay production-clean: use accessible contrast, stable dimensions, concise labels, and predictable navigation rather than decorative flourishes.
